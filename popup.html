Before you continue

To find community-submitted segments, SkipTube sends the current YouTube video ID and your enabled categories to SponsorBlock. SponsorBlock receives that request and ordinary network information such as your IP address. SkipTube stores preferences and cached segments in your browser; it has no server.

SkipTube uses this information only to retrieve and skip the segments you choose. A segment is sent to SponsorBlock only if you review and confirm a public submission.
