# Community Code of Conduct

SkipTube contributors aim to make participation respectful, useful, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including during disagreement.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Share relevant context, give actionable feedback, and accept that others may have different experience or priorities.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, or browsing data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when this code is violated. Once the repository is public, use its configured moderation or reporting controls. No individual contact address is published in this local preparation.
